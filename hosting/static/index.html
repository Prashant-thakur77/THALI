Intel Physical AI · Bimanual VLASpeechmatics

Thali — two arms that set your table by voice

Say what you want and two SO-101 arms open the drawer, lay out cutlery and plate, hold the mug and pour — for people who can speak but can't reach. Speechmatics realtime speech · local Qwen2-VL planner on Intel OpenVINO · deterministic safety verifier with a hash-chained audit log · learned skills with a scripted-IK fallback. Everything except speech recognition runs on an Intel Core i7-13650HX laptop (no NPU).

▶ Open Thali Live — drive the simulator yourself served from the lab machine; if it does not answer, the machine is off — the video below shows the same loop
